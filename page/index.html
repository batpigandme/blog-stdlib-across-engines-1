stdlib's digest in this browser

This page runs stdlib's erf and ldexp on 4,053 inputs, in this browser's JavaScript engine, and prints each result as the bits of a double. It then hashes those lines, so equal digests mean equal bits on every line, and compares them with a reference taken on Node.js. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The bit-for-bit digest may differ for one reason the page can't rule out: the inputs include a signalling NaN, and engines encode that NaN their own way. The NaN-equal digest, which counts every NaN as NaN whatever its bits, is the one expected to match.
